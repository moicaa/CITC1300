Sepultura's History

Sepultura's Wiki

Sepultura is the first band formed by Max Cavalera. The name is Brazilian Portuguese for "grave." Max Cavalera, while playing a very important role in founding the band, left in 1996, with Igor Cavalera leaving a decade later in 2006. They disbanded this year, in 2026, after a farewell tour.

Information sourced from Wikipedia.
