Ne-Net's History

Kazuaki Takashima, the main designer and creator of Ne-Net, has an interesting past. He studied at Bunka Fashion College, a Japanese vocational school. Upon graduation, he worked as a designer at the well known brand Issey Miyake. He then created Ne-Net, and the brand started operations under A-net Inc. After years of new clothes being released and semi-consistent runway appearances, Ne-Net eventually had to stop operations. The brand now exists only in archives, with clothing being resold mostly by third party collectors.

Kazuaki Takashima

Kazuaki Takashima has worked on projects aside from Ne-Net. One of them is called "This is not a cat." Takashima left his brainchild Ne-Net in 2020, a year before it's unfortunate end, to start his new project "This is not a cat." This new project leaves the casual attire of Ne-Net behind. "This is not a cat" focuses on art exhibitons and handmade clothing. While clothing may still be purchased from "This is not a cat," the pieces drop more periodically and less frequently.
