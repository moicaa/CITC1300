Various Types of Seals

There are 34 different species of seals, but only 18 of them are "true" seals. True seals lack ears. Some seals that would be considered "eared" seals instead of true seals are sea lions and fur seals. These are my 5 favorite types of seals.

Fun Seal Facts ^_^

The smallest wild seals are around only 1 meter long, while the largest can be up to 5 meters long. Female seals also live 5 years longer than male seals, with female seals living an average of 30 years, and male seals living around 25 years.
